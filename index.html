    reminders

    Reminders show in the popup and are spoken, while Plumb is open. "Every N minutes" only runs during your working hours.

    focus timer

    While a focus block runs, every alert is paused: posture, water, reminders, light and the rest. Tracking carries on as normal. During the timer's breaks, Plumb's own "take a break" and "move" prompts stay quiet too.

    end of day

    Once a day at this time, while tracking is running, Plumb tells you your wrap is ready. You can open it any time from the top of the page.

    calls

    Anything on your calendar with a Meet, Zoom or Teams link counts as a call. Tracking carries on as normal; only voice and on-screen alerts pause. Does nothing if no calendar is linked.

    workstation setup

    step 1 of 5

    A one-off walkthrough for your desk: where your eyes sit against the camera, how far you are from the screen, lighting, then a few desk basics a webcam can't measure for you, ending with calibrating your good-posture baseline. Sit the way you'd normally work, then click next.

    You don't need to start the camera first -- the next step turns it on for you. (For a quick look before a video call, use are you camera ready? instead -- that one is about how you look to others, this one is about how the desk suits you.)

    camera height & screen distance

    Monitor's top edge at or slightly below eye level, screen about 50–70cm away (roughly 20–28 inches -- a bit less than a full arm's reach). Sit naturally and watch the readings update as you move.

    Camera needs to be on for a live reading -- nothing's recorded, same as everywhere else in Plumb.

    Most webcams sit at the top edge of the screen, so where your eyes land in the picture tells you how your eyes compare to the top of the screen. Assumes the camera faces straight out -- if you've tilted it, the reading shifts. Raise or lower yourself or the screen and watch the dots move.
